## Key Takeaways (from Corey Ganim's thread, not the transcript)

- Entry: asks network for intros to $10M+ companies and asks owners for advice, not a sales call.
- Paid assessment from day 1 ($1,000, recently $5,000); survey department heads first, then record 30-minute calls with each.
- Rank workflows by ROI using client numbers (19 workflows, est. $255K-$285K back in year one).
- Build working demos on the client's real documents and show the whole C-suite on one call.
- Quote 3 tiers at prices higher than comfortable; deal closed in about 6 weeks at $15K/month for 6 months, then a $5K+/month maintenance plan.
