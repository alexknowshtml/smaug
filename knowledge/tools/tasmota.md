Alternative open-source firmware for ESP8266 and ESP32 devices that gives easy webUI configuration, OTA updates, timers and rules automation, and entirely local control over MQTT, HTTP, serial or KNX. The tweet highlights flashing cheap smart plugs to cut them off from the vendor cloud.

## Key Features

- Integrates with Home Assistant, Domoticz, openHAB, Node-RED and KNX via MQTT; can run fully standalone
- Native Matter support on ESP32 (Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, Home Assistant) and bridging of ESP8266 devices into Matter
- 150+ supported sensors and peripherals over I2C, SPI, 1-Wire and analog
- Controls lights (single-channel to RGBCW and addressable strips), shutters and blinds, thermostats, and reads smart meters
- Protocol bridging for IR, RF, DALI, Modbus, RS-485, CAN, LoRa, HDMI-CEC, Telegram and SMTP; Zigbee coordinator and BLE gateway modes
