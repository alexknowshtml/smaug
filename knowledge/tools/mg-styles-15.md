Fifteen motion-design styles, each a 10-second film made by Claude Opus 5.5 writing code from a single prompt. Every film ships with style notes, the full prompt and complete source.

## Key Features

- No image or video generation: pictures are SVG, Canvas, WebGL and Three.js (Blender for one 3D film), and audio is synthesized by an audio.py per film.
- Pipeline: a web page exposes renderAt(t), headless Chrome captures frames, ffmpeg muxes the MP4, and a second AI reviews against a rubric for two revision rounds.
- English and Chinese READMEs; MIT licensed.
