Liquid AI's cookbook of examples, end-to-end tutorials and apps for its open-weight Liquid Foundation Models (LFM) and the LEAP SDK, aimed at laptops, mobile and edge devices. @helloiamleonie bookmarked it for two fine-tuning notebooks that teach supervised fine-tuning of Liquid's d1-3B decision model, one using Unsloth and one using Hugging Face TRL, both runnable in about 30 minutes on a free-tier GPU.

## Key Features

- Notebook `sft_for_decision_models_with_unsloth.ipynb`: prepare training data and fine-tune the d1-3B decision model with Unsloth
- Notebook `sft_for_decision_models_with_trl.ipynb`: the same workflow with Hugging Face TRL
- Desktop, browser (WebGPU), Android and iOS example apps built on LFMs (invoice parser, audio transcription, home assistant, etc.)
- Road Decider example: a pixel-art racer where the d1 decision model dodges traffic
- About 2.7k stars, Jupyter Notebook, Liquid AI-maintained
