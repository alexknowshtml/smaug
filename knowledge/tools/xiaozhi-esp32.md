An MCP-based AI voice chatbot firmware for ESP32 boards. The XiaoZhi chatbot uses large models such as Qwen and DeepSeek as the voice interaction entry point, and controls other devices and services through the MCP protocol. The repo had roughly 33k stars at bookmark time (the tweet cited 30.8k).

## Key Features

- Networking over Wi-Fi, wired Ethernet, USB RNDIS, or Cat.1 4G, with boards able to switch between Wi-Fi and 4G
- Offline voice wake-up via ESP-SR with customizable wake words
- WebSocket and MQTT+UDP transports; Opus audio streaming with ASR + LLM + TTS pipelines or realtime end-to-end voice models
- Speaker recognition (3D Speaker), OLED/LCD displays with emoji, and camera vision input on supported boards
- Device-side MCP (speaker, LED, servo, GPIO) and cloud-side MCP (smart home, PC desktop operation, knowledge search, email)
- Supports ESP32, C3, C5, C6, S3, and P4 chips across 138 board directories and 171 release variants; requires ESP-IDF v6.0.1 or later
